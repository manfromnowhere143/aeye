## Executive verdict

The four-coordinate Aeye thesis is strong and should proceed as a research program. The
strongest architecture is not “Hawkeye on Pearl” and not a flat receipt. It is a typed
evidence graph in which `WORK`, `EXECUTION`, `SEMANTICS`, and `DEMAND` each have their own
relation, assurance class, coverage, assumptions, verifier, and failure state.

Pearl is valuable but narrower than both proposals initially assumed. It supplies
conjecture-dependent evidence for one accepted, committed matrix-work instance. It does
not bind that instance to a full model/request/output. The proposed public `hash_b` census
is genuinely interesting, but the initial plan's load-bearing byte-for-byte checkpoint claim is
contradicted by the pinned runtime: vLLM fusion, TP slicing, and MoE packing transform the
checkpoint before Pearl hashes the contiguous slab. Deterministic reconstruction may still
be possible and is now E-000, not an accepted premise.

Hawkeye is scientifically important but not the most complete current arithmetic model.
MMA-Sim covers a broader instruction/architecture surface. Both are empirical semantics
adapters, not proofs of job execution or physical hardware identity. Neither covers the
full float glue of modern inference.

The central correction is cryptographic: a trace root or lineage fold makes a provider's
story non-equivocating, not true. Strong `EXECUTION` begins only with post-commit sampled
recomputation, an honest-challenger protocol, a sound proof of the exact relation, or an
explicitly conditional attestation path. Therefore Aeye v0 can issue commitment receipts
without a proof system, but it cannot mark `EXECUTION=satisfied` on that path.

## What survives from the initial proposal

- the four-way separation;
- the observation that Pearl exposes operand commitments worth measuring;
- two-level aggregate/request receipt scope for continuous batching;
- public census buckets and asymmetric interpretation;
- demand as a policy-priced event, never intrinsic utility;
- no monolithic proof-system dependency in the commitment-only fast path;
- rigorous negative fixtures and benchmark baselines.

## What is rejected or materially changed

| Proposal | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `hash_b` is a raw public checkpoint tensor | `contradicted` | Runtime fusion, TP/EP slicing, and MoE packing intervene. |
| L2 eliminates stripped/non-inference miners | `rejected` | A miner can replay a registered slab with garbage `A`, repeat one slab, or omit the model. |
| L3 lineage root closes activation substitution | `rejected` | It binds a self-reported trace; no transition relation is checked. |
| Quantization boundary is the protocol granularity | `modified` | Useful adapter surface, but caches, attention, sampling, retries, and fused operations require a semantic event DAG. |
| Hawkeye verifies the float glue | `rejected` | It models selected MMA instructions; not norms, RoPE, softmax, cache logic, or sampling. |
| Freivalds/GKR gives genuinely sub-1% semantics | `unsupported` | No output commitment/challenge relation or measurement establishes that number; Freivalds alone needs committed `C` and does not cover the execution DAG. |
| One 32-byte V4 fold closes execution lineage | `rejected` | Consensus-binding is not truth; version namespace is already inconsistent between live V3 and draft PIP-3. |
| Mainnet census must precede all other work | `modified` | E-000 precedes it; census and a semantic-DAG prototype then answer orthogonal questions. |
| Aeye v0 needs no proof system | `qualified` | True for `C0` receipts; false for cryptographically satisfied execution. |

## Six decision questions

`Q1 | ANSWER: FALSE (as authored) | CONFIDENCE: high |` The exact runtime `w_q` is hashed,
but it is not generally one released checkpoint tensor byte-for-byte. Public reconstruction
through a pinned loader/fusion/parallelism recipe is a new, unresolved hypothesis.

`Q2 | ANSWER: BOTH-IN-PARALLEL | CONFIDENCE: high |` After E-000, the census measures
registered-weight participation while the semantic-DAG prototype tests the actual Aeye
execution thesis. Neither substitutes for the other.

`Q3 | ANSWER: OTHER | CONFIDENCE: high |` Use a per-execution-event DAG with explicit tensor,
request-membership, cache, attempt, and output edges. Quantization boundaries are one event
type, not the protocol invariant.

`Q4 | ANSWER: YES-AT-L3 | CONFIDENCE: high |` A proof system is not mandatory specifically;
an independent verifier is. Commitment-only L3 must remain `unknown/commitment_only`.

`Q5 | ANSWER: NO-DIFFERENT-FIELD | CONFIDENCE: high |` Do not propose a V4 fold now. First
specify the statement to be checked and resolve Pearl's version collision. Any future
extension should be a domain-separated receipt/evidence root and must not pretend the field
validates its contents.

`Q6 | ANSWER: PARTIAL-ESCAPE | CONFIDENCE: high |` Public slab reconstruction makes one
random-weight substitution class observable. It does not close registered-slab replay,
garbage activations, partial execution, output grafting, or private model ambiguity.

## Strongest implementable architecture

1. `C0` fast lane: canonical manifests, job/output/trace binding, Pearl work references,
   demand events, and honest `commitment_only` results. Target <1% only here.
2. `A1` audit lane: unpredictable post-commit path/tile checks with explicit worst-case
   detection probability.
3. `O2` optimistic lane: independent duplicate/referee path with one-honest-challenger and
   availability assumptions.
4. `Z3` strong lane: DeepProve/zkGPT-class full or layerwise relation for supported
   quantized models; report circuit semantics, not native GPU semantics.
5. Arithmetic lane: cross-validated Hawkeye/MMA-Sim profiles for covered instructions,
   independent of optional physical attestation.
6. Demand lane: signed/fresh authorization and optional escrow/independence policy; never a
   utility proof.

## Novel contribution, deflated

Aeye is not a new SNARK, tensor commitment, hardware simulator, or PoUW construction. Its
credible novelty is the disciplined composition of an external work clock, a stable
serving-event relation, multi-strength verification backends, arithmetic conformance, and
policy-scoped demand into one non-conflating receipt—plus a public instrument for measuring
which registered runtime weight slabs appear in Pearl certificates if E-000 succeeds.

## Go conditions

Proceed with architecture validators and E-000. Do not build a product or make a security/
performance claim until the pre-implementation gate is accepted. Kill or reframe the Pearl
census if runtime slabs cannot be independently reconstructed. Keep Aeye even if the census
fails: the four-coordinate receipt protocol does not depend on Pearl, and Pearl remains a
replaceable `WORK` adapter.
