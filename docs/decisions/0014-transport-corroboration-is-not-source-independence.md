# ADR-0014: Transport Corroboration Is Not Source Independence

Status: `accepted`; implemented for the E-000 pre-candidate registry; pending fresh
reviewer readback  
Date: 2026-09-21

## Decision

E-000 assigns checkpoint-registry trust tiers only through a reviewer adjudication bound
to exact retained evidence. The owner-side artifact may not assign its own tier.

The version-0 registry prequalifier implements `T0` observation and `T1` candidacy only:

- `T0` requires an immutable repository revision and, for every weight shard named by the
  pinned index, one complete stream whose byte length and SHA-256 equal the frozen LFS
  identity.
- A `T1` candidate requires `T0` plus a second, separately initiated complete retrieval
  of every shard. The two receipts must identify different HTTPS source origins,
  final-delivery hosts, TLS sessions, retained IP addresses, AS numbers, and operator
  organisations. Redirect reuse, range or resumed transfers, derivation from local bytes,
  incomplete shard coverage, missing transcript material, and owner-supplied path labels
  fail closed.
- `T1` exists only after a separate reviewer adjudicates every receipt under the frozen
  rejection order and publishes a record bound to the registry and prequalification
  digests. CLI reports retain both the exact registry-file digest and a deterministic
  parsed-content digest. Structural eligibility is not that adjudication.
- A shared publisher or object store is permitted at `T1` only because `T1` addresses
  transport tampering. That dependence must be disclosed. The resulting statement is
  limited to transport corroboration under single-publisher trust.
- `T2` is never inferred by the version-0 prequalifier. It requires a separate contract for
  reproducing the quantized checkpoint from a different publisher's base model and a
  published transformation recipe.

The registry contains metadata, public-safe receipt projections, raw-receipt digests,
tensor inventory, recipe identity, and the finite tensor-parallel degree set. Query
parameters and credentials are excluded from public URLs while the exact private receipt
remains content-addressed. The registry contains no model-weight bytes and no candidate
height, certificate, or `hash_b`.

## Rationale

The predecessor E-000 freeze defines `T1` as a pinned revision, retrieval receipts,
per-file digests, and a second retrieval over an independent network path. It also states
that `T1` detects tampering in transit rather than poisoning at the source. A mirror name,
proxy label, matching `ETag`, or publisher signature therefore cannot establish either
path independence or source authenticity.

This distinction matters to E-000. A malicious but internally consistent publisher can
supply the same bytes over both paths and still satisfy transport corroboration. The
experiment may later show that a Pearl `hash_b` equals a byte-string associated with that
registry entry; it may not infer that the publisher identity is true, that the checkpoint
was independently reproduced, or that a complete model executed.

The prequalifier retains the first applicable transport failure for each shard under the
pre-registered rejection order. A truncated or mismatching proxy response is evidence
about that route, not evidence against the checkpoint and not a zero-valued observation.

## Consequence

- The maximum owner-observed tier is `T0`. Version 0 can emit
  `eligible_for_review` with candidate tier `T1`; it cannot assign T1.
- The candidate tier statement is generated by the prequalifier rather than copied from
  the registry producer. It has no authority until the separate adjudication accepts it.
- Every shard must prequalify; one corroborated shard cannot upgrade a multi-shard entry.
- Shared-effective-origin retrievals carry the qualifier `single-publisher trust`.
- Any `T2`, source-authentication, independent-publication, or verified-weights language
  attached to a T1 candidate is rejected.
- A registry below T1 keeps E-000 at its pre-candidate `BLOCKED` gate. It does not select
  or inspect a candidate and does not produce a scientific result.
