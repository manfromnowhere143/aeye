> **2026-09-21 adversarial addendum.** E-009 remains a supported toy interface result,
> but its coded path is dominated at this scale: 832 field multiplications to authenticate
> preprocessing versus 90 for exact replay in the retained operation-count model. The
> source-pinned [PAB-1 note](PEARL_ACTIVATION_ORIGIN_BINDING_2026-09-21.md) now specifies a

Status: `observed` E-009-R1 result; pending independent human review and architecture acceptance  
Verdict: **CONDITIONAL-GO / FIRST CLAIM-BEARING TOY RESULT / FRONTIER SYSTEM VALUE UNPROVEN**

## Executive verdict

Aeye now contains a technically serious research thesis and one successful, preregistered,
claim-bearing toy execution. It is not yet a demonstrated breakthrough, a deployable
protocol, or evidence that the industry should adopt it.

The strongest contribution is a precise **Pearl work-to-execution coupling problem**. Aeye
preserves a version-pinned Pearl `WORK` certificate, requires an independent execution
relation to expose the same native operand roots on a causal path to a bound output, and
keeps arithmetic semantics and demand non-fungible. The construction has a projection
property, a concrete non-implication witness, a conditional soundness bound, a machine-
checked positive interface fixture, and a rejecting mismatched-root fixture.

E-009-R1 is the first constructive witness for the execution side of that statement. A
separate implementation replayed 13/13 causal events—five coded linear relations and eight
exact boundaries—and rejected all eight preregistered mutations. The conditional
five-relation coded-audit bound is approximately `4.99e-25` (`2^-80.73`). Pearl evidence
remained deliberately synthetic, so `WORK` is still `unknown`; `DEMAND` is `unsupported`.

This is a broader and now partly instantiated claim surface than Pearl alone. It is not a
claim that Aeye has better work security, consensus, economics, or performance than Pearl.

## What changed this verdict

### 1. Pearl is now treated as two protocols

The retained INT certificate-v3 snapshot and September 2026's proposed FP8
certificate-v4 are separated by parser, arithmetic, device, wire, and hardness-assumption
identifiers. The FP8 specification is substantive: post-noise quantization, selected-tile
bit-exact verification, and jackpot checks make Pearl's native work statement more precise.
Its central quantized-subspace premise remains an explicit informal assumption, and the
retained implementation change was an open PR at the cutoff.

Any analysis that casually combines the old INT runtime, FP8 security discussion, and a
generic “current Pearl” claim is invalid.

### 2. Aeye stopped pretending receipt placement is proof binding

Evidence now distinguishes `receipt_job_id` from `native_public_inputs`. Pearl natively
authenticates a work context and operand commitments; it does not authenticate an Aeye job,
trace, output, or demand event. An execution backend must authenticate those Aeye values
itself. The validator joins the two relations only through equal native operand roots.

This closes a common composition error: writing the same identifier into two envelopes and
calling it cryptographic coupling.

### 3. The demand binding is no longer circular

The prior draft described a demand event that bound `job_id` while its own digest was also
an input to `job_id`. Aeye now commits a pre-authorization `intent_id`; the demand event
authenticates that intent; the final `job_id` then binds the event root. A known-bad fixture
rejects intent mutation.

### 4. The current 2026 frontier is integrated without laundering claims

- [Maverick](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10264v1) supplies an important
  information-theoretic delegated matrix-vector verification primitive with transparent
  preprocessing and low server overhead, but not automatically a public transferable
  whole-execution receipt.
- [DeepProve](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1112) remains the strongest retained
  end-to-end quantized LLM relation, at substantial prover cost.
- [zkComposer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08095v1) and
  [Sound Debloating](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10149v1) offer credible ways
  to parallelize or reduce a correctly specified proof relation; neither repairs omitted
  public inputs.
- [Jolt Atlas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17452v1) offers a lookup-centric,
  streaming ONNX proving path, but its circuit semantics remain distinct from native
  serving and GPU semantics.

No retained system establishes Aeye's four coordinates at once.

## Is it revolutionary?

`unknown`, and that remains the only honest answer.

The formulation is frontier-worthy because it identifies and formalizes a missing
relation between useful-work evidence and an actual serving execution. The negative
results are equally important: work cannot imply execution, circuit correctness cannot
imply physical effort, arithmetic compatibility cannot imply device origin, and a
task-oblivious transcript cannot reveal independent demand.

E-009-R1 now instantiates `R_X(subject,h_A,h_B)` for one finite-field, hard-attention toy
relation and survives its preregistered local adversarial controls. That is a genuine
research result, not merely architecture prose. It becomes important infrastructure only
if the relation extends to actual Pearl roots, realistic inference semantics, private or
bounded-disclosure transcripts, and competitive total cost at serving scale. Those
conditions have not been met.

## Is the work ready for expert review?

Yes, within its stated ceiling. The technically credible basis for a serious review is:

- the analysis respects Pearl's strongest current formulation rather than attacking an
  obsolete caricature;
- it preserves Pearl's native claim and assumption instead of relabelling them;
- it presents an exact additional relation, public-input boundary, reduction target, and
  counterexample;
- it identifies where the new FP8 design helps Aeye and where it remains orthogonal;
- it includes executable rejection cases and states what has not been built.
- it includes a retained preregistration, public transcript, evidence receipt, separate
  verifier implementation, exact conditional bound, 8/8 mutation result, and complete toy
  cost surface rather than only a proposed experiment.

An expert is still likely to ask, correctly: Can the toy backend consume actual Pearl
roots? Who authenticates `Q=G^T M` without recomputing the whole matrix? What prevents
subject grinding? How are softmax, normalization, batching, and real cache reuse covered?
What is the privacy cost? Does the construction win against a complete proof baseline on
the same workload? Aeye does not yet answer those questions experimentally.

The work is ready for **technical criticism with a concrete artifact to attack**. It is
not evidence for adoption, deployment, security, performance, or any individual or
institutional endorsement.

## Defensible novelty

The following is `proposed` novelty pending literature review and independent assessment:

1. a four-coordinate, no-laundering result vector specialized to inference evidence;
2. native-public-input discipline that distinguishes receipt scope from authenticated
   relation inputs;
3. a Pearl-preserving `WORK∧EXECUTION` coupling profile with projection and strictness
   witnesses;
4. semantic event-DAG and coverage rules for batching, caches, retries, and shared work;
5. a two-stage demand binding plus an impossibility boundary for unauthenticated demand;
6. one replayable validation surface where each consequential rule has a known-bad case.

RATS roles, evidence/result envelopes, CBOR/COSE protection, transparency logs, PoUW,
zkML, delegated verification, and arithmetic simulators are prior work and are not claimed
as Aeye inventions.

## Present evidence

`observed` in this repository:

- retained, content-addressed primary sources and standards with a validated ledger;
- five JSON Schemas covering receipts, reports, sources, experiments, and negative
  fixtures;
- a deterministic validator for bindings, ordering, evidence typing, assumptions,
  coverage, retained-source integrity, and reviewer independence;
- 18 replayed fixtures: three valid structural examples and 15 designed failures, all
  producing the expected result;
- a deterministic Reed–Solomon/Vandermonde coded-MVM interface demonstrator that accepts
  the correct finite-field relation and rejects the retained altered-output case;
- E-009-R1, retained before execution by digest, with 13/13 events, five authenticated
  coded relations, 80/80 accepted equations, eight exact relations, and 8/8 preregistered
  mutation rejections under a separately implemented verifier;
- E-009's four-coordinate result: `WORK=unknown`, `EXECUTION=satisfied/sampled`,
  `SEMANTICS=satisfied/hybrid` for the exact toy profile, and `DEMAND=unsupported`;
- local toy measurements: 29.458 µs median execution baseline, 112.041 µs trace/subject,
  1,461.354 µs challenge plus responses, 1,940.771 µs independent replay, and an
  85,584-byte provider bundle versus a 222-byte canonical output; an explicitly post-hoc
  split measured 703.584 µs client challenge derivation and 699.687 µs provider response
  construction without upgrading the preregistered result;
- 19 passing unit tests;
- proposed, blocked E-000 and E-008 manifests for certificate-v3 runtime-slab
  reconstruction and certificate-v4 verifier/B200 parity; E-009 is `supported` only for
  its frozen toy question;
- formal composition, standards crosswalk, version firewall, and coupling profile.

The repository validator remains structural evidence only. E-009's separate verifier adds
relation-level evidence for its toy execution, but verifies no Pearl certificate,
signature, hardware origin, production performance statement, or real demand event.

## Blocking evidence

The following remains `unknown` or `blocked`:

1. E-000 has no frozen non-self-mined certificate, predating checkpoint revision, or
   independently reproduced runtime transformation.
2. No Pearl mainnet census has been preregistered or run.
3. No backend implements `R_X` for an ordinary transformer, native serving stack, batched
   request, real cache-reuse path, or actual Pearl operand roots; E-009 covers only its
   frozen 13-event toy DAG.
4. No retained target-device experiment establishes FP8 certificate-v4/B200 parity.
5. No arithmetic-profile experiment covers the full inference surface.
6. E-009 measures local toy provider/verifier/byte cost and now has an exploratory
   party-level split, but that split was post hoc and does not measure production serving,
   setup amortization, network, privacy, failure recovery, or a complete comparator.
7. No independent cryptographer, systems reviewer, Pearl engineer, or operator has
   accepted the architecture.

## Decision

Proceed from the successful toy slice to the smallest discriminating scale-up:

1. freeze and execute E-000 for the historical certificate-v3 question;
2. specify and reproduce certificate-v4 test vectors separately;
3. authenticate `Q=G^T M` once against the exact model/layout root so an online verifier
   need not touch the full matrices, and quantify setup amortization;
4. replace the deterministic hard-attention profile with a frozen quantized attention,
   normalization, cache, decode, and batched-membership relation;
5. consume actual native Pearl operand roots through a version-specific clean-to-work
   bridge, then attack replay, garbage activation, omitted transition, output graft,
   challenge grinding, and cross-request membership;
6. benchmark provider, client/challenger, verifier, network, storage, setup, privacy, and
   recovery separately against a named complete proof baseline;
7. obtain independent cryptographic and Pearl-engineering review before comparative or
   external adoption claims.

The proposed successor is documented as APC-1 with an E-010 manifest. It is blocked until
a proof/evidence lane, workload, privacy policy, challenge model, independent verifiers,
amortization counts, and complete comparator are frozen and accepted.

Stop or reframe the Pearl coupling if no backend can expose and verify the same operand
roots without unacceptable duplication, privacy loss, or serving overhead. Preserve the
four-coordinate evidence calculus even if Pearl is replaced: its separation result does not
depend on one work protocol.
